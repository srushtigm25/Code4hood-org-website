eS.T.E.A.M.E.D. Academy & Maker Space (Homeschool/Charter School)

Our Get eS.T.E.A.M.E.D. Pathways program is designed for youth in 4th – 12th grade. Each pathway includes hands-on learning projects that they truly enjoy! As students master each tech skill, they advance to the next level. Every S.T.E.A.M.E.D class builds on the one before it, meaning that students graduate with a vast mastery of S.T.E.A.M.E.D skills and a portfolio full of cool, working web pages, samples, designs, apps, certificate of completion and digital badges.
