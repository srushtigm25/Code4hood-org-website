Our  Mission & Vision

Who we are and what our goals are.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire and equip youth with the knowledge, tools, and experiences needed to excel in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM). Through hands-on learning, mentorship, and collaborative programs, we cultivate curiosity, build resilience, and nurture entrepreneurial mindsets. We are committed to bridging opportunity gaps by offering inclusive, community-based pathways that prepare young people for future careers and empower them to create solutions for their communities.

Our Vision

Vision Statement Empowering youth to become the next generation of innovators, leaders, and changemakers by providing equitable access to STEAM education, fostering creativity, and developing lifelong skills for success in a dynamic world.

Introduction Statement

Code 4 Hood Edu (dba eS.T.E.A.M.E.D. Academy & Maker Space) is a youth serving community based 501c (3) non-profit organization dedicated to empowering youth through STEAM education, youth workforce development & entrepreneurship, and life, leadership and social emotional skill development. Founded in 2015 and rebranded in 2024 we serve ALL youth ages 12 – 24 throughout Sacramento, CA; giving priority enrollment to programs, services and events to underserved, at risk, aging out of foster care, re-entry marginalized youth and youth who live and/or attend school in the following priority neighborhoods Council District 5: Oak Park/South Sacramento, Council District 4: Marina Vista/Seavey Circle, and Council District 6: Lemon Hill/Fruitridge providing innovative learning pathways, wrap around services that improve quality of life and digital literacy opportunities. Our programs, events and services are designed to foster leadership, creativity, and life, college and career readiness, preparing participants for success in today’s rapidly evolving economy.

Code 4 Hood Edu primary office is in South Oak Park, within the HUD Sacramento Promise Zone at 4625 44th Street #9 Sacramento, CA 95820.
