Next ARMed Workforce

Next ARMed Workforce builds a Sacramento-based ecosystem to educate, train, and connect the region's most in-demand next workforce, our youth, our young adults, and our mature reentry adults.

This initiative builds on Code 4 Hood's long-standing STEAM education platform, extending it into a full-lifecycle talent pipeline, from early K-12 intervention through young adult career entry, and reskilling for adults reentering the workforce after layoffs, incarceration, or extended absence. The focus: education, workforce pathways, prototyping, and robotics integration for K-12 youth, young adults, and reentry, reskilling, and mature adults.

For the Community & Youth This Means

Stronger career pathways in AI, robotics, and advanced manufacturing for students, educators, and adult learners

Direct connection to high-quality local jobs

Increased investment in infrastructure and innovation across Oak Park and South Sacramento

Renewed pathways for reentry and reskilling adults returning to sustainable careers

Four Pillars

K-12 Early Intervention Programs

Introducing AI, robotics, and STEAM to K-12 scholars, building resilience and opportunity from the earliest touchpoint.

  • School district partnerships for out-of-school-time programming to prevent academic dropouts, gang involvement, and violence, and reduce the likelihood of justice involvement
  • Above the STEM camp series
  • In-classroom and after-school robotics programs via eSTEAMED Academy

Workforce Development & Career Pathways

A K-through-career talent pipeline

  • Pathways TEC deep learning workforce program, via Code 4 Hood
  • AI literacy programs
  • Adult upskilling opportunities

Research & Innovation

Applied R&D through partnerships and shared access to compute and industry expertise, a clear path from lab to market.

  • Direct access to partners for research and prototyping projects
  • A dedicated network for experimentation and industry collaboration
  • Claude Corps Host Site in Oak Park with 4 AI Fellows, via Code 4 Hood

Partnerships & Infrastructure

Building the connective tissue between talent, employers, and place.

  • High-quality connections with employers hiring in AI, robotics, and advanced manufacturing
  • Opportunity-ecosystem partnerships with workforce boards, universities, local employers, and city and county government
  • Partnership with AI companies such as Anthropic, ChatGPT, Poppy AI, Microsoft, Google, NVIDIA, etc.
