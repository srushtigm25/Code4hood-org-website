Safe Spaces Program

Stabilization. Support. Pathways Forward.

The Safe Spaces Program provides youth with wraparound support, navigation, and access to resources that help remove barriers to their success.

We recognize that it can be difficult for a young person to focus on school, employment, personal development, or their future when basic needs are unmet. Safe Spaces helps youth address those immediate needs, build stability, and connect to opportunities that help them move forward.

Who We Serve

Safe Spaces supports youth who may be:

  • Experiencing housing instability or homelessness
  • Facing food, clothing, transportation, or other basic-needs challenges
  • Justice-involved or reentering the community
  • Transitioning from foster care or other systems of care
  • Disconnected or at risk of becoming disconnected from school or employment
  • Navigating challenging life circumstances or barriers to stability
  • In need of additional support, resources, or guidance

What Safe Spaces Provides

Our Safe Spaces Program uses a stabilization-first, youth-centered approach that meets young people where they are and helps them identify the resources, support, and next steps they need to move forward.

Youth Navigation & Personalized Support

Youth receive individualized support from a Youth Navigator who helps identify needs, develop goals, connect to resources, coordinate services, advocate when needed, and provide consistent follow-up.

Stabilization & Basic Needs Support

We help youth address immediate barriers that may prevent them from successfully participating in school, employment, training, or other opportunities.

Support may include access to:

  • Housing support and referrals
  • Food and basic necessities
  • Clothing and personal care items
  • Transportation assistance
  • Education and academic support
  • School re-engagement
  • Employment and workforce resources
  • Mental health and wellness referrals
  • Life skills and independent living resources
  • Community services and additional referrals based on individual needs

From Stabilization to Opportunity

Safe Spaces is more than meeting an immediate need. Our goal is to help young people move from stabilization to opportunity.

Once immediate barriers are addressed, Youth Navigators help participants identify their next step. Youth may transition into Code 4 Hood’s broader ecosystem of STEM education, workforce and career pathways, and youth development and empowerment programs, or be connected to trusted community partners when another organization can better meet their needs.

The goal is to make sure young people don’t simply receive a referral, they have support navigating what comes next.

Who Can Refer Youth?

Youth can enter Safe Spaces through referrals from trusted community and system partners, as well as directly from youth and families.

Referral sources may include:

  • Schools and school districts
  • Probation and reentry programs
  • Foster care and social service agencies
  • Community-based organizations
  • Youth-serving organizations
  • Families and caregivers
  • Self-referrals
