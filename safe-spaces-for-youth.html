Safe Spaces Program

Stabilization. Support. Pathways Forward.

At Code 4 Hood Edu, our Safe Spaces Program is designed to support youth who need more than just a program—they need stability, guidance, and a trusted place to land.

Safe Spaces serves youth who are:

  • Justice-involved or reentering the community
  • Transitioning out of foster care
  • Experiencing homelessness or housing instability
  • At-risk or navigating challenging life circumstances
  • From historically marginalized communities

What Safe Spaces Provides

Our Safe Spaces Program is a stabilization-first model that meets youth where they are and walks alongside them as they rebuild, refocus, and move forward.

Personalized Support Navigation

Each youth is paired with a dedicated Youth Navigator who provides one-on-one guidance, advocacy, and consistent support.

Safe, Welcoming Environment

We create a physically and emotionally safe space where youth feel seen, heard, and supported without judgment.

Stabilization First Approach

Before expecting participation, we focus on helping youth stabilize their immediate needs—because growth can’t happen without a strong foundation.

Access to Critical Resources
  • Housing support and referrals
  • Mental health and wellness services
  • Education and school re-engagement
  • Workforce development and job readiness
  • Life skills and independent living support

From Stabilization to Opportunity

Once youth are stabilized, they gain access to the full ecosystem of Code 4 Hood programs.

Safe Spaces is not just a program—it’s the entry point to transformation.

STEAM Education Pathways
Workforce Development & Career Training
Entrepreneurship Programs
Leadership & Life Skills Development

Who Refers Youth?

Youth are referred through trusted systems, community partners, and direct family support so that they can access stabilization and next-step opportunities as early as possible.

  • Schools and school districts
  • Probation and reentry programs
  • Foster care and social service agencies
  • Community-based organizations
  • Self-referrals and family referrals

The Drop

The Drop is more than a program—it's a safe space where youth can show up as their full selves, feel supported, and simply be. Designed as a welcoming environment for teens, The Drop provides a consistent place to connect, decompress, and engage in meaningful activities after school and on weekends.

Grounded in our MIND16 Mental Wellness Framework, The Drop intentionally integrates social-emotional learning, healing-centered practices, and mental health awareness into every experience. Youth participate in guided conversations, reflection activities, and wellness workshops that help them process emotions, build resilience, and develop healthy coping strategies—all in a safe, judgment-free space.

What The Drop Offers

Open Lab Time & Creative Expression

Space for youth to explore technology, games, creative projects, and activities that allow them to express themselves freely and decompress.

Social-Emotional Learning

Guided conversations, reflection activities, and wellness workshops that help youth process emotions and build resilience.

Identity & Wellness Exploration

Opportunities for youth to explore identity, manage stress, strengthen their voice, and develop healthy coping strategies.

Peer Connection & Mentorship

Caring, trained staff and mentors who create trauma-informed environments where every young person feels seen, heard, and valued.

The Drop Experience

In addition to open lab time, creative expression, technology, and games, The Drop offers opportunities for youth to explore identity, manage stress, and strengthen their voice through peer connection and mentorship. Caring staff and mentors are trained to create trauma-informed environments where every young person feels seen, heard, and valued.

The Drop is where community meets care—where youth build confidence, find support, and develop the tools they need to navigate life both inside and outside the program.
