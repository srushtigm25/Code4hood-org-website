Youth Development & Empowerment

Code 4 Hood’s Youth Development & Empowerment programs help young people build confidence, strengthen leadership skills, support emotional well-being, and prepare for healthy, successful futures.

Own Your Future

Own Your Future is Code 4 Hood’s youth empowerment and prevention program designed to help young people make informed decisions about their health, choices, and future goals.

Through interactive workshops, mentorship, service-learning, and community projects, youth explore substance use prevention, harm reduction, leadership development, and personal responsibility while building the confidence to make healthy choices and take ownership of their future.

Futures: West Sacramento

Futures: West Sacramento is a youth development initiative designed to support young people in building leadership skills, exploring career interests, and strengthening their connection to their community.

Through mentorship, workshops, and community-based activities, youth gain opportunities to develop life skills, engage in leadership experiences, connect with local resources, build confidence, and create a stronger vision for their future.

MIND16 – Mental Health Initiative

MIND16 is Code 4 Hood’s youth wellness and mental health initiative focused on helping young people develop emotional awareness, coping skills, and resilience.

Through workshops, guided discussions, and peer engagement activities, youth learn about stress management, emotional health, mindfulness, and self-care while developing the tools needed to maintain healthy mental and emotional well-being in a supportive environment.

Youth Healing Circles

Youth Healing Circles provide safe, supportive spaces where young people can explore healing, build resilience, and connect with others through shared experiences.

These circles combine guided discussions, trauma-informed mentorship, and healing through movement to support youth navigating stress, trauma, relationships, and personal growth.

  • Movement-based healing such as dance, yoga, and mindfulness
  • Open dialogue and guided reflection
  • Peer support and community building
  • Social-emotional skill development

Life Coaching for Teens

Trauma-Informed Life Coaching for Teens is a personalized support service designed to help young people navigate life’s challenges while building confidence, resilience, and a clear path forward.

Through a trauma-informed lens, trained mentors provide a safe, supportive environment where teens feel seen, heard, and empowered.

  • Build self-confidence and emotional awareness
  • Develop healthy coping strategies and decision-making skills
  • Set personal, academic, and career goals
  • Strengthen communication and relationships
  • Navigate school, family, and life transitions

Youth Wall Street

Youth Wall Street is a cultural, social justice, life, and leadership skill development program focused on entrepreneurship, life skills, health and wellness, BIPOC history, financial literacy, communication and relationship skills, and crime and substance abuse prevention. The program helps youth strengthen their voice, expand their opportunities, and build the knowledge needed to thrive in their communities and future careers.

Love & Logic: Building Healthy Relationships

Love & Logic is a dynamic, youth-centered program designed to help young people build healthy relationships, strengthen decision-making skills, and develop emotional awareness. Rooted in the evidence-based Love Notes curriculum, this program creates a safe and engaging space for youth to explore real-life topics such as communication, boundaries, self-respect, peer pressure, and making informed choices.

Through interactive discussions, role-playing scenarios, and guided activities, participants learn how to recognize healthy vs. unhealthy relationships, manage emotions, and think critically about the impact of their decisions. The program also emphasizes goal setting, personal responsibility, and understanding how today's choices shape future opportunities.

Love & Logic goes beyond conversation—it equips youth with practical tools they can apply in their everyday lives, empowering them to build stronger relationships with peers, family, and themselves.

Crowned in Confidence Sr.

Crowned in Confidence Sr. is an advanced empowerment and leadership development program designed for older youth ready to step into their voice, purpose, and power. This program creates space for deeper conversations around identity, self-worth, leadership, and navigating real-world challenges with confidence and clarity.

Participants engage in workshops, group discussions, and personal development activities that focus on goal setting, decision-making, communication, and leadership presence. Youth are challenged to think critically, build resilience, and develop the confidence to advocate for themselves and others.

Crowned in Confidence Sr. is about more than confidence—it's about ownership. Ownership of your story, your voice, and your future. Participants leave equipped with the mindset, tools, and leadership skills to show up boldly in school, in their communities, and in life.
