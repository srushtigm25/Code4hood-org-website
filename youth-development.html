Youth Development & Empowerment

Code 4 Hood’s Youth Development & Empowerment programs help young people build confidence, strengthen leadership skills, support emotional well-being, and prepare for healthy, successful futures.

Own Your Future

Own Your Future is Code 4 Hood’s youth empowerment and prevention program designed to help young people make informed decisions about their health, choices, and future goals.

Through interactive workshops, mentorship, service-learning, and community projects, youth explore substance use prevention, harm reduction, leadership development, and personal responsibility while building the confidence to make healthy choices and take ownership of their future.

Futures: West Sacramento

Futures: West Sacramento is a youth development initiative designed to support young people in building leadership skills, exploring career interests, and strengthening their connection to their community.

Through mentorship, workshops, and community-based activities, youth gain opportunities to develop life skills, engage in leadership experiences, connect with local resources, build confidence, and create a stronger vision for their future.

Crowned in Confidence Jr.

Crowned in Confidence Jr. is an empowerment and confidence-building program designed for younger youth to explore identity, self-worth, and personal growth in a supportive, nurturing environment.

Through workshops, group discussions, creative activities, and peer engagement, youth develop self-confidence, strengthen communication skills, build healthy relationships, and discover their unique strengths. The program creates a safe space for young people to express themselves, connect with peers, and begin building the confidence and resilience they'll carry throughout their lives.

Crowned in Confidence Sr.

Crowned in Confidence Sr. is an advanced empowerment and leadership development program designed for older youth ready to step into their voice, purpose, and power.

This program creates space for deeper conversations around identity, self-worth, leadership, and navigating real-world challenges with confidence and clarity.

Participants engage in workshops, group discussions, and personal development activities that focus on goal setting, decision-making, communication, and leadership presence. Youth are challenged to think critically, build resilience, and develop the confidence to advocate for themselves and others.

Crowned in Confidence Sr. is about more than confidence. It is about ownership of your story, your voice, and your future. Participants leave equipped with the mindset, tools, and leadership skills to show up boldly in school, in their communities, and in life.

Level Up & Thrive

Youth Wellness & Resilience

Level Up & Thrive is our youth wellness initiative designed to help young people strengthen their emotional awareness, coping skills, resilience, and overall well-being.

Through interactive workshops, guided discussions, peer engagement, and wellness activities, youth explore topics such as stress management, emotional wellness, mindfulness, self-care, healthy coping strategies, and building resilience.

The program also incorporates Youth Healing Circles, creating space for young people to connect, reflect, share experiences, and build community in a supportive environment.

Through Wellness Through Movement, youth explore the connection between physical and emotional well-being through activities such as dance, yoga, stretching, and intentional movement.

Level Up & Thrive gives young people multiple ways to connect, express themselves, develop healthy coping tools, and build the skills they need to navigate life's challenges and thrive.

Adulting 101

Real-Life Skills for Life After High School

Adulting 101 helps young people learn the practical, everyday life skills they don't always get taught in school but definitely need for adulthood.

Through hands-on workshops and real-world activities, youth learn how to navigate responsibilities such as banking, budgeting and financial literacy, grocery shopping, cooking, basic car care, changing a tire, managing bills, understanding credit, household basics, and making smart financial and everyday decisions.

The program also introduces skills related to independent living, personal responsibility, time management, communication, problem-solving, and navigating everyday adult situations.

From opening a bank account to planning a grocery budget or learning what to do when you get a flat tire, Adulting 101 is about building the confidence and practical know-how young people need to take care of themselves and successfully navigate life after high school.

My Future Plans

College, Career & Life Readiness Coaching

My Future Plans is a personalized, one-on-one coaching program that helps young people build confidence, set meaningful goals, and create a clear plan for life after high school.

Working individually with a supportive coach, youth develop a personalized plan focused on their academic progress, college or career goals, personal development, and overall readiness for adulthood.

Youth receive support to:

  • Set personal, academic, college, and career goals
  • Create a plan for high school completion and life after graduation
  • Explore college, training, employment, and career pathways
  • Strengthen decision-making, communication, and self-advocacy skills
  • Build confidence, emotional awareness, and healthy life skills
  • Navigate school, family, relationships, and life transitions
  • Identify actionable next steps and stay accountable to their goals

Whether a young person is planning for college, career training, employment, entrepreneurship, or still figuring out what's next, My Future Plans gives them a trusted coach and structured support to turn their ideas for the future into a plan they can begin working toward today.

Love & Logic

Healthy Relationships Program

Love & Logic is our healthy relationships program designed to help young people build stronger relationships, make informed decisions, and develop the skills needed to navigate relationships with confidence and self-awareness.

Using the evidence-based Love Notes curriculum, youth explore real-life topics including healthy and unhealthy relationships, communication, boundaries, self-respect, peer pressure, emotional awareness, decision-making, and personal responsibility.

Through interactive discussions, role-playing, and guided activities, participants learn to recognize healthy relationship behaviors, communicate effectively, establish personal boundaries, manage emotions, and understand how the choices they make today can impact their future.

The program also encourages goal setting and future planning, helping youth think about how relationships connect to their personal, academic, and career goals.

Love & Logic gives young people practical tools to build healthy relationships with themselves, their peers, families, and future partners while making choices that support the life they want to create.

Youth Wall Street

Financial Empowerment, Social Justice & Leadership

Youth Wall Street is a culturally responsive youth development program that helps young people build the knowledge, confidence, and life skills needed to navigate money, relationships, systems, and their communities.

Through interactive workshops and real-world discussions, youth explore financial literacy, entrepreneurship, social justice, civic engagement, cultural history, leadership, communication, healthy relationships, and crime and substance-use prevention .

Participants learn practical financial skills such as budgeting, saving, banking, understanding credit, and making informed financial decisions , while also engaging in conversations about their rights, interacting with law enforcement, navigating community systems, advocacy, and issues that impact young people and their communities.

Youth Wall Street encourages young people to know their worth, understand their voice, make informed decisions, and develop the financial and leadership skills to create opportunities for themselves and their communities.

IMPACT Crew

Inspiring Minds, Purpose, Action, Community & Teamwork

Youth Leadership & Work Experience

IMPACT Crew is a paid youth leadership and work experience program for a cohort of 12 young people ages 12–19 who are ready to build their leadership skills, gain real-world experience, and make an impact in their community.

Throughout the program, youth participate in leadership development, job readiness, communication, teamwork, event planning, and personal development workshops. Activities such as StrengthsFinder and True Colors help participants better understand their individual strengths, leadership styles, and how they work effectively with others.

IMPACT Crew members put their skills into action by helping to plan, develop, and host SacTown Youth X events and experiences. Through this hands-on work, youth gain practical experience in event planning, outreach, decision-making, problem-solving, community engagement, and working as part of a team.

Youth receive a stipend for their participation while gaining valuable leadership and work experience they can carry into school, employment, and future opportunities.

Each funded program cycle welcomes a new cohort of young leaders, giving more youth the opportunity to discover their strengths, find their voice, build their skills, and make an IMPACT.

Next Move

Violence Prevention & Intervention

Next Move is a community-based violence prevention and intervention program designed to connect youth with support, mentorship, and positive opportunities during critical moments in their lives.

Through partnerships with probation, law enforcement, schools, community organizations, and other youth-serving systems, the program works to identify and engage young people who may be at increased risk of violence, justice-system involvement, or other circumstances that can impact their safety and future.

Using a youth-centered, harm-reduction approach, participants are connected to trusted mentors, positive peer experiences, workshops, leadership development, life skills, and structured activities that provide alternatives to violence and strengthen their connection to their community.

The program also serves as a bridge to longer-term support. Youth can be connected to Code 4 Hood's broader ecosystem of Safe Spaces stabilization and navigation services, workforce and career pathways, STEM programs, wellness initiatives, and youth development opportunities, as well as additional community resources when needed.

Through initiatives such as Sac Town Youth Pop-Ups, West Sac Community Futures, and targeted intervention programming, Next Move creates opportunities to reach young people where they are, build trusted relationships, and help them move toward greater safety, stability, connection, and opportunity.
