Monthly Giving

Join the Village. Create Consistent Impact.

Becoming a monthly donor means you're part of something bigger—a community of supporters committed to long-term change. Your recurring gift allows us to plan, grow, and consistently serve youth year-round.

No amount is too small—consistent giving creates powerful impact over time.

As a monthly giver, you help:

  • Sustain ongoing programs and safe spaces
  • Expand access to underserved youth
  • Provide consistent mentorship and resources
  • Build a stronger, more stable future for our organization

Give monthly. Grow impact. Change lives.

Sponsoring a Youth

Make It Personal. Make It Impactful.

Sponsoring youth is one of the most direct ways to change a life. Your sponsorship ensures that a young person can fully participate in one of our programs—from start to finish—without financial burden.

You're not just covering a cost—you're opening a door.

What your sponsorship can support:

  • Full program enrollment for one youth
  • Access to mentorship and hands-on learning experiences
  • Transportation, meals, and necessary materials
  • Confidence, skills, and real-world exposure

Want to go further? Sponsors can receive updates on program impact and the journey they're helping support.

BDOG (Big Day of Giving)

Give Big. Make an Immediate Impact.

Big Day of Giving (BDOG) is a powerful 24-hour giving movement that brings our community together to support the future of our youth. On this special day, your donation goes even further helping us expand programs, reach more students, and deepen our impact across the communities we serve.

Your BDOG gift supports hands-on STEAM learning, workforce development, mentorship, and safe spaces for youth who need it most. It's a chance to rally together and make a meaningful difference fast.

Your donation helps us:

  • Serve more youth across our programs and communities
  • Expand STEAM, workforce, and leadership opportunities
  • Provide access to safe spaces and mentorship
  • Invest in resources, technology, and program growth

One day. One community. A lasting impact.
