Prevention & Intervention

The Blueprint

Blueprint is Code 4 Hood’s 16-week program for justice-involved youth and young people in transition, designed to help them leave with a real plan for what comes next. Through weekly 90-minute sessions, participants build job readiness skills, learn to manage money, and practice the life skills it takes to live independently. Each young person completes a capstone reentry plan and earns a stipend for finishing the program. Code 4 Hood then stays alongside them, coordinating with probation officers and partners as they transition home, so no one starts their next chapter alone.

The First 90 Days

First 90 Days is Code 4 Hood’s reentry program for justice-involved youth, built to meet young people on the day they’re released from custody and walk with them through their first 90 days home. Each participant is paired with a credible mentor with lived experience who understands the road ahead. Together with Code 4 Hood’s team, participants get help with job placement, housing through connections to transitional housing, and wraparound services such as IDs, benefits, transportation, and health care. Those first three months set the foundation for everything that follows, and First 90 Days makes sure no young person faces them alone.

Pathways to Success

Pathways to Success is Code 4 Hood’s violence prevention program for youth ages 10 to 19, funded by the City of Sacramento’s Children’s Fund. Based at our Oak Park Studio at 4625 44th Street, the program gives young people a safe, consistent place to grow, with classes, workshops, mentoring, and life coaching alongside supportive services that help them and their families thrive. Participants build the skills, relationships, and confidence to make positive choices and plan for their future.

West Sacramento Futures

Futures: West Sacramento is a youth violence prevention and intervention initiative that empowers young people to become leaders, strengthen their connection to their community, and help create safer environments for themselves and their peers.

Through mentorship, leadership development, workshops, peer engagement, and community-based activities, youth build skills in conflict resolution, healthy decision-making, communication, violence prevention, and community leadership while exploring their personal, educational, and career goals.

In partnership with the City of West Sacramento, Futures creates a Community of Practice where youth voice and lived experience help shape solutions, strengthen community connections, and promote positive change.

Futures gives young people a seat at the table and the tools to help build safer, stronger communities while creating a positive vision for their own future.

SacTown Youth Pop-Ups

SacTown Youth Pop-Ups is Code 4 Hood’s violence prevention events series, planned and hosted by our adult mentors alongside the Impact Crew, our youth leadership team. Once or twice a month, usually on a Friday or Saturday, we bring young people together for safe, positive experiences like game nights, dinner and movie nights, and trips to Sacramento Kings games. Events take place across Sacramento County, with a focus on South Sacramento and Oak Park, and are free for youth ages 12 to 19. Every pop-up is a chance to have fun, connect with caring mentors, and build community.
