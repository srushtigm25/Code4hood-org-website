Impact

Code 4 Hood believes lasting change happens through collaboration, community leadership, and shared learning. Beyond our direct youth programs, we work alongside nonprofit partners, community leaders, schools, and employers to strengthen youth opportunity pipelines across Sacramento.

Through our communities of practice, partnerships, events, and community initiatives, we are building ecosystems that support youth development, workforce readiness, and community empowerment.

Communities of Practice

Code 4 Hood participates in collaborative learning networks that bring together youth-serving organizations, community leaders, and workforce partners to strengthen opportunities for young people across Sacramento.

One of our key collaborations is through the Pathways to Partnership initiative led by PRO Youth & Families, where Code 4 Hood works alongside 11 youth-serving organizations to bridge the gap between youth workforce development programs and local employers.

Through this collaboration, partners share resources, align programming, and create coordinated pathways that help young people move from education and training into meaningful career opportunities. By working together, we are strengthening Sacramento’s youth workforce ecosystem and expanding opportunities for the next generation.

Partnerships

Strong partnerships allow Code 4 Hood to expand our impact and reach more youth across Sacramento communities. We work with nonprofit organizations, community leaders, and local initiatives to deliver programs, host community events, and provide resources that support youth and families.

Key partnerships include collaborations with organizations such as the Salvation Army, Color the Block, and community initiatives like Sac Town Mardi Gras and the Think Twice Initiative. Together, we create opportunities for youth to access mentorship, workforce development experiences, and community-based learning environments.

Through these partnerships, we are able to deliver programs in trusted community spaces while strengthening the network of organizations committed to youth success.
