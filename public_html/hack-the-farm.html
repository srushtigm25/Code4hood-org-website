Hack the Farm Easter Egg Hunt

Welcome to Hack the Farm 2025 Sacramento's Only STEAM Infused Easter Egg Hunt! Join us at Estate Farms, Excelsior Road, Sacramento, CA, USA for a day filled with technology, agriculture, and Easter fun. Get ready to put your problem-solving skills to the test as you hunt for Easter eggs with a twist. This event is perfect for those who love science, technology, engineering, arts, and math. Don't miss out on this unique opportunity to blend traditional Easter activities with modern innovation. See you there!
