What we do

Code 4 Hood (dba eS.T.E.A.M.E.D. Academy & Maker Space) is a youth serving community based 501c (3) non-profit organization dedicated to empowering youth through STEAM education, youth workforce development & entrepreneurship, and life, leadership, and social emotional skill development. Founded in 2015 and rebranded in 2024 we serve ALL youth ages 10 – 24 throughout Sacramento, CA; giving priority enrollment to programs, services and events to underserved, at risk, aging out of foster care, re-entry marginalized youth and youth who live and/or attend school in the following priority neighborhoods Council District 5: Oak Park, Council District 4: Marina Vista-Seavey Circle, and Council District 6: Lemon Hill. By providing innovative learning pathways, wrap around services that improve quality of life and digital literacy opportunities. Our programs, events and services are designed to foster leadership, creativity, and life, college, and career readiness, preparing participants for success in today’s rapidly evolving economy.

Code 4 Hood primary office is in South Oak Park, within the HUD Sacramento Promise Zone at 4625 44th Street #9 Sacramento, CA 95820

How we do it

We are committed to bridging the technology divide and giving ALL youth a real advantage: academically, socially, and in S.T.E.M. Our goal is not only for youth to learn, make and create but to heal, gain hope and gain access to life changing opportunities

We do this by providing:

  • Structured and comprehensive S.T.E.A.M.E.D. curriculum in safe spaces with trauma informed mentors, teachers, and staff
  • Exceptional teachers who experts in their fields
  • Wrap around services to nurture the whole individual youth.
  • Interactive, project-based learning via classes, camps, workshops, and leagues
  • Pathways to real world training, internships, and certifications

Our  Mission & Vision

Who we are and what our goals are.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire and equip youth with the knowledge, tools, and experiences needed to excel in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM). Through hands-on learning, mentorship, and collaborative programs, we cultivate curiosity, build resilience, and nurture entrepreneurial mindsets. We are committed to bridging opportunity gaps by offering inclusive, community-based pathways that prepare young people for future careers and
empower them to create solutions for their communities.

Our Vision

Empowering youth to become the next generation of innovators, leaders, and changemakers by providing equitable access to STEAM education, fostering creativity, and developing lifelong skills for success in a dynamic world.
