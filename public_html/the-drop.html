The Drop

The Drop is more than a program—it's a safe space where youth can show up as their full selves, feel supported, and simply be. Designed as a welcoming environment for teens, The Drop provides a consistent place to connect, decompress, and engage in meaningful activities after school and on weekends.

Grounded in our MIND16 Mental Wellness Framework, The Drop intentionally integrates social-emotional learning, healing-centered practices, and mental health awareness into every experience. Youth participate in guided conversations, reflection activities, and wellness workshops that help them process emotions, build resilience, and develop healthy coping strategies—all in a safe, judgment-free space.

What The Drop Offers

Open Lab Time & Creative Expression

Space for youth to explore technology, games, creative projects, and activities that allow them to express themselves freely and decompress.

Social-Emotional Learning

Guided conversations, reflection activities, and wellness workshops that help youth process emotions and build resilience.

Identity & Wellness Exploration

Opportunities for youth to explore identity, manage stress, strengthen their voice, and develop healthy coping strategies.

Peer Connection & Mentorship

Caring, trained staff and mentors who create trauma-informed environments where every young person feels seen, heard, and valued.

The Drop Experience

In addition to open lab time, creative expression, technology, and games, The Drop offers opportunities for youth to explore identity, manage stress, and strengthen their voice through peer connection and mentorship. Caring staff and mentors are trained to create trauma-informed environments where every young person feels seen, heard, and valued.

The Drop is where community meets care—where youth build confidence, find support, and develop the tools they need to navigate life both inside and outside the program.
