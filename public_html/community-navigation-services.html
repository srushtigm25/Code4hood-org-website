Community Navigation Services

Connecting People to Resources, Support & Care

Our Community Navigation Services help youth, individuals, and families find and connect to the community resources and support they need to improve stability and well-being.

Using a Community Health Worker (CHW) and care coordination approach, our Navigators work alongside participants to understand their needs, identify available resources, make connections and referrals, and help them navigate systems that can sometimes feel difficult or overwhelming.

Navigation may include assistance connecting to resources for housing, food, transportation, healthcare, behavioral health and wellness, education, employment, benefits, family support, and other social and community needs.

Our role is to help people navigate—not replace—the services provided by healthcare providers, government agencies, and community organizations. When a need is identified, we help participants understand their options, connect with the appropriate provider or resource, and follow up to help reduce barriers to accessing support.

For youth participating in our Safe Spaces Program, navigation can also serve as a bridge from immediate stabilization needs into Code 4 Hood's broader ecosystem of youth development, STEAM education, workforce development, and career pathway opportunities.

Sometimes finding help is the hardest part. Our Navigators help people figure out where to start and what comes next.
