Part-Time

STEM Instructor

$50/hour or $100 per 2-hour class

Code 4 Hood is looking for an energetic, dependable STEM Instructor to lead hands-on after-school classes in coding, robotics, and game development for elementary and middle school students.

You’ll teach from our curriculum, guide students as they build, code, and troubleshoot their projects, and manage a class of up to 20 students.

Classes run after school, generally between 3:00 and 5:30 p.m., about 2–3 days a week at Sacramento-area school and community sites.

You don’t need to know every platform we use. A strong STEM foundation, experience working with youth, and a willingness to learn are what matter most.

Paid training and a monthly phone stipend are included.

Part-Time

STEAM Classroom Assistant

$25.00/hour

Our STEAM Classroom Assistants support instructors in after-school classes for elementary and middle school students.

You’ll help set up and put away materials, work one-on-one with students on their projects, and help keep the classroom positive, safe, and focused.

This is a great fit for college students, aspiring educators, and anyone who enjoys technology and working with young people.

No teaching experience required, and training is provided.

Apply on Indeed

Requirements for Both Roles

  • Experience working with youth
  • Reliable transportation
  • Completion of required clearances before starting, including:
    • Live Scan background check
    • TB clearance
    • Mandated reporter training
    • CPR/First Aid
