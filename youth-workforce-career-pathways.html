Pathways TEC

Pathways Training & Employment Center

Pathways TEC is Code 4 Hood’s workforce development and career pathway designed to help young people move from career exploration to career readiness, skills development, and meaningful employment or entrepreneurship.

Through hands-on learning, workforce development, mentorship, career exploration, and real-world experiences, youth build the confidence, professional skills, and practical knowledge needed to succeed in today’s evolving economy.

Our three-tiered pathway meets youth where they are and provides opportunities to progress as they build their skills and identify their goals:

Job Club → Spark U → iWork Immersive

Job Club provides the foundation through job-readiness workshops, mentorship, career exploration, and essential workplace skills.

Spark U – Career Exploration University takes youth deeper through a 12-week experience that allows them to explore career pathways, industries, and opportunities while continuing to strengthen their workforce readiness skills.

iWork Immersive is our intensive, hands-on workforce pathway where participants move from exploring careers to developing industry-specific skills through an earn-while-you-learn model, preparing them for employment and other career opportunities.

Together, these programs create a pathway that helps young people explore what’s possible, prepare for what’s next, and build the skills and experience to move forward.

Future Ready Job Club

Get Ready. Get Hired. Stay Employed.

Job Club is our 12-week job readiness program designed to help young people build the foundational skills, confidence, and tools they need to successfully enter the workforce.

Through interactive workshops and individualized support, youth develop resumes and cover letters, practice interviewing, complete career and behavioral assessments, learn workplace expectations, strengthen communication skills, build a professional social media presence, and prepare for the realities of starting and keeping a job.

Job Club can be completed independently or alongside SPARK U, allowing youth to build job-readiness skills while simultaneously exploring careers and industries that match their interests and strengths.

Participants who successfully complete the program are eligible to receive a program stipend and receive support connecting to entry-level employment opportunities in areas such as retail, food service, customer service, recreation, and other first-job opportunities.

And our support doesn’t stop once a young person gets hired. Through our 90-Day Employment Mentorship, we continue working with youth and employer partners during those critical first months on the job—helping young people navigate challenges, build strong workplace habits, and successfully maintain employment.

The goal of Job Club isn’t simply to help youth get their first job. It’s to help them be ready to keep it and build from it.

SPARK U Career Exploration University

SPARKU is a 40-hour, cohort-based career exploration experience that helps young people discover career possibilities, explore their interests, and begin identifying pathways they may want to pursue.

Rather than traditional job training, SPARK U is focused on career discovery and exposure. Youth explore different industries through hands-on labs, career guest speakers, build experiences, mentorship, behavioral and work-readiness assessments, and cohort-based learning.

SPARK U works together with Job Club, our job-readiness program. Youth may participate in both programs concurrently building essential workplace skills through Job Club while using SPARK U to explore where those skills could take them. Youth may also participate in SPARK U as a stand-alone career exploration experience.

Participants who successfully complete SPARK U are eligible to receive a program stipend while gaining valuable exposure to industries, careers, training opportunities, and professionals they may not otherwise encounter.

  • FiberU — Fiber Optics, Broadband & Telecommunications Careers

  • InnovateU — Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Emerging Industries

The goal of SPARK U is for every young person to leave with a stronger understanding of what they are interested in, what career pathways are available to them, and the confidence to believe those opportunities are within reach.

For young people ready to take the next step, SPARK U can serve as a bridge into iWork Immersive, where career exploration becomes intensive, hands-on skills development and real-world work experience.

iWork Immersive

Learn. Work. Earn.

iWork Immersive is an eight-month, hands-on occupational training and workforce preparation program that helps young people move from career exploration into real-world career experience.

Built around a project-based, earn-while-you-learn model, participants are placed in an iWork Immersive Work Lab or with an employer partner where they gain practical, on-the-job experience while developing the technical and professional skills needed to enter an in-demand career.

Depending on the pathway, participants may prepare for careers such as Cybersecurity Analyst, IT Support Technician, AI Engineer, AI Data Center Technician, Healthcare IT Specialist, Surgical Robotics Technician, Solar Panel Installer, EV Technician, Clean Air Technician, Decommissioning Technician, Green Construction Technician, Farming Robotics Technician, and other emerging occupations.

Throughout the program, youth receive hands-on occupational training, paid work-based learning experiences, mentorship, industry exposure, and preparation for applicable industry certifications or credentials. Participants also receive a program completion certificate upon successfully completing their iWork Immersive experience.

We work directly with local employers and industry partners to connect training to real workforce needs and help participants transition from training into employment and continued career advancement.

iWork Immersive is where career exploration becomes career experience—and young people begin building the skills, credentials, connections, and experience to launch their next step.

Youth Work Experience (YWEX)

Youth Work Experience (YWEX) is Code 4 Hood's on-the-job training and employment pathway that gives young people the opportunity to gain meaningful, real-world work experience while preparing for long-term employment.

Code 4 Hood serves as a work experience host site for workforce development agencies and career academies throughout the Sacramento region, creating structured training opportunities for youth and young adults generally ages 16–30, depending on the eligibility requirements of the sponsoring workforce program.

Participants complete 180+ hours of hands-on, on-the-job training in real workplace roles while developing professional skills, building their résumé, and gaining experience they can take into their next job.

Work experience is layered with Code 4 Hood's Job Club and Spark U programming, providing participants with additional career readiness, mentorship, career exploration, workplace skills, and employment preparation.

After successfully completing their work experience, participants transition into our Job Incubator, where we work to connect them with entry-level employment opportunities through participating employers and workforce partners.

YWEX creates a bridge from training to experience to employment—giving young people an opportunity to learn on the job, build their skills, and take the next step toward meaningful employment.

The Launchpad Lab

An Entrepreneurship & Incubator Academy

The Launchpad Lab is where ideas turn into action. This hands-on entrepreneurship and incubator academy empowers youth to think like innovators, build like founders, and lead like CEOs.

Participants learn how to take an idea from concept to creation, developing business plans, branding, products, and real-world solutions to everyday problems.

Through interactive workshops, mentorship, and collaborative team challenges, youth explore topics such as financial literacy, marketing, product development, and pitch creation. By the end of the program, participants will have built their own business concept or project and gain the confidence to present it to peers, community members, and potential supporters.

What You'll Learn & Explore
  • Financial Literacy: Understand budgeting, revenue models, funding, and how to manage finances for a business idea or startup venture.
  • Marketing & Branding: Learn how to build a brand identity, create marketing strategies, and connect with a target audience.
  • Product Development: Design, prototype, and develop a business idea or product from concept through creation and testing.
  • Pitch & Presentation: Develop compelling pitches and presentation skills to share ideas with investors, supporters, and the community.
Who Should Join?

Whether you want to start a business, launch a side hustle, or simply strengthen your leadership and creative thinking skills, The Launchpad Lab equips youth with the mindset and tools to create their own opportunities and shape their future.

The program welcomes young people with entrepreneurial ideas, youth interested in innovation and creative problem-solving, those looking to build leadership and business skills, and students exploring career paths in business and entrepreneurship.

Join The Launchpad Lab and turn your ideas into reality. Learn from mentors, collaborate with peers, and build the confidence to lead your own future.
