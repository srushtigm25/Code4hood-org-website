Afterschool Clubs

Free After-School Programs for Sacramento Youth

Code 4 Hood runs two free after-school clubs for youth ages 10 to 14 in the Sacramento City Unified School District area.

Open Monday through Friday from 3 to 5 p.m., our clubs offer homework help, free snacks and meals, and daily activities, which could be sports, arts and crafts, STEM, or open computer lab time.

Each club serves up to 12 youths at a time with a 1-to-6 staff-to-youth ratio.

Our clubs are drop-in programs, not licensed child care facilities. Youth may walk over from school and leave on their own to walk home.

To sign up your child, email us at hello@code4hood.org .
