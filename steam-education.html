STEAM Education

Code 4 Hood’s STEAM Education programs introduce youth to the exciting worlds of science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics through hands-on learning, creative exploration, and real-world problem solving. Our programs are designed to build digital literacy, critical thinking, and technical skills while helping youth explore emerging technologies and future career opportunities. Through workshops, afterschool classes, and community learning spaces, students gain the confidence to become creators, innovators, and problem-solvers.

STEAM Studio

The STEAM Studio introduces youth to science, technology, engineering, art, and math through interactive classes, camps, and clubs designed to make learning hands-on, creative, and fun.

Students participate in engaging programs including game development, robotics and engineering, digital art and animation, coding and programming, 3D printing, and Junior CAD. Each experience gives youth an opportunity to learn new technology, build technical skills, and apply what they learn through creative, project-based activities.

The STEAM Studio encourages students to think, create, build, experiment, and problem-solve while developing confidence with technology and discovering new interests.

Whether students are building a robot, designing a game, creating digital artwork, writing their first lines of code, or designing a 3D model, the STEAM Studio makes STEM learning an experience they can see, touch, build, and bring to life.

Maker Space

The Maker Space is a hands-on creative learning environment where children and youth can make, explore, design, and create using technology, digital tools, art, and their imagination.

Our space is equipped with 3D printers, laser cutters, laptops, Cricut machines, creative workstations, art materials, maker tools, and other digital fabrication equipment that allow young people to turn their ideas into real-world projects. Youth can explore everything from digital design and engineering to art, technology, prototyping, and creative problem-solving.

Through programs like Maker Space Mondays, youth have dedicated time to explore their own interests and work on projects at their own pace, with supportive instructors and educators available to provide guidance along the way.

Whether they're designing something from scratch, learning a new tool, building a prototype, or simply experimenting with an idea, our Maker Space gives young people the space, tools, and support to bring their creativity to life.

Digital Literacy

The Digital Literacy Program helps youth build the essential technology skills they need to succeed in school, prepare for the workforce, and confidently navigate an increasingly digital world.

Through hands-on learning, youth explore computer fundamentals, internet safety and digital citizenship, productivity tools, online collaboration, coding and technology platforms, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence (AI), and introductory machine learning concepts.

Participants learn not only how to use technology, but how to use it safely, responsibly, creatively, and effectively. As technology continues to change the way we learn, work, communicate, and solve problems, our program helps youth develop the confidence and practical skills to keep pace.

From everyday digital skills to emerging technologies like AI and cybersecurity, the Digital Literacy Program prepares young people to be informed technology users, creative problem-solvers, and future-ready learners.

CyberPatriot

CyberPatriot is the nation's largest youth cyber education program, designed to inspire K–12 students to pursue careers in cybersecurity, technology, and other STEM disciplines.

Code 4 Hood is bringing CyberPatriot programming to our youth, providing hands-on opportunities to build cybersecurity skills while learning how to protect and defend computer systems and networks.

Through the National Youth Cyber Defense Competition, students work in teams to identify cybersecurity vulnerabilities, secure virtual systems, solve real-world challenges, and strengthen their critical thinking, teamwork, and technical skills.

CyberPatriot gives young people an exciting pathway to explore cybersecurity careers, develop practical technology skills, and experience what it takes to become the next generation of cyber professionals.
