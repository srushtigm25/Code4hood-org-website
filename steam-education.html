STEAM Education

Code 4 Hood’s STEAM Education programs introduce youth to the exciting worlds of science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics through hands-on learning, creative exploration, and real-world problem solving. Our programs are designed to build digital literacy, critical thinking, and technical skills while helping youth explore emerging technologies and future career opportunities. Through workshops, afterschool classes, and community learning spaces, students gain the confidence to become creators, innovators, and problem-solvers.

Get STEAMED

GET STEAMED is Code 4 Hood’s hands-on afterschool enrichment program that introduces youth to STEAM through interactive classes and creative projects.

Students participate in engaging classes that may include:

  • Game development
  • Robotics and engineering
  • Digital art and animation
  • Coding and technology exploration
  • Maker and design challenges

GET STEAMED encourages students to think creatively, build problem-solving skills, and discover new interests while having fun learning with their peers. The program helps youth build confidence while exploring the possibilities of STEAM education.

Maker Space

The Drop Teen Tech Center provides a safe and creative space where teens can explore technology, digital media, and innovation in a supportive environment. Inspired by the national Teen Tech Center model, this program allows youth to experiment with creative technologies while developing valuable digital skills.

Participants can explore areas such as digital design, coding, media production, and technology-based projects while collaborating with mentors and peers.

The Drop is designed to give young people a place where they can learn, create, and build confidence through technology and creativity.

Digital Literacy

Code 4 Hood’s Digital Literacy Programs help youth build the foundational technology skills needed to succeed in school, the workforce, and everyday life.

These workshops introduce participants to essential digital skills including:

  • Computer fundamentals
  • Internet safety and responsible technology use
  • Productivity tools and online collaboration
  • Introduction to coding and technology platforms

By strengthening digital literacy, we help youth gain the confidence and skills needed to navigate today’s technology-driven world and access future career opportunities.

CyberPilot

CyberPilot introduces youth to the fundamentals of cybersecurity and digital safety, helping them understand how technology systems work and how they can be protected.

Through interactive learning experiences, participants explore topics such as:

  • Online safety and responsible digital behavior
  • Introduction to cybersecurity concepts
  • Understanding cyber threats and digital protection
  • Careers in cybersecurity and technology

CyberPilot is designed to help youth develop awareness of cybersecurity while sparking interest in one of the fastest-growing career fields in technology.
