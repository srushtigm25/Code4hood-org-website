Digital Access & Community Technology Services

Code 4 Hood is committed to closing the digital divide by providing community members with access to technology, digital resources, and essential tools needed to succeed in today’s digital world.

Our Digital Access & Community Technology Services are designed to support youth, families, job seekers, and community members who may not have reliable access to computers, internet connectivity, or digital support services.

Through these services, we aim to ensure that everyone in our community has the opportunity to connect, learn, apply for opportunities, and build digital skills.

“Technology access is opportunity. Our goal is to ensure every member of our community has the tools to connect, learn, and grow.”

Digital Resource Hub

The Digital Resource Hub serves as a centralized place where community members can access important online resources, technology support, and digital learning opportunities.

The hub will provide access to curated tools and information related to:

  • Job searches and workforce development resources
  • Educational platforms and digital learning tools
  • Scholarship and training opportunities
  • Technology tutorials and digital literacy guides
  • Community programs and services

The goal of the Digital Resource Hub is to help individuals easily find the resources they need to learn, grow, and succeed in a technology-driven world.

Low-Cost Internet Services

Reliable internet access is essential for education, employment, and everyday communication. Code 4 Hood works to connect families and community members with low-cost or subsidized internet service programs available through local and national providers.

Through this initiative, we help community members:

  • Identify affordable internet service options
  • Apply for low-cost broadband programs
  • Understand eligibility for digital connectivity assistance
  • Gain the tools needed to stay connected for school, work, and essential services

Our goal is to ensure that families in our community have the connectivity needed to access opportunities and stay engaged in today’s digital economy.

Free Community Copy Center

The Free Community Copy Center provides access to essential printing and copying services for youth and community members who may not have access to these resources at home.

Services may include:

  • Printing resumes and job applications
  • Copies of school assignments or educational materials
  • Printing forms and documents needed for housing, employment, or services
  • Community resource materials

By offering this service, Code 4 Hood helps remove barriers that prevent individuals from completing important tasks related to education, employment, and community services.

Open Computer Lab

The Open Computer Lab provides a welcoming space where community members can access computers and the internet for educational, professional, and personal use.

Community members can use the lab for activities such as:

  • Job searches and online applications
  • Homework and research
  • Digital literacy learning
  • Resume writing and workforce training
  • Accessing online resources and services

The Open Computer Lab is designed to create a safe and supportive environment where individuals can develop digital skills and access the tools they need to succeed.
